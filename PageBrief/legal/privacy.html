PageBrief Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

What We Collect

PageBrief processes the text content of pages you choose to summarize. Account email and usage metrics may be stored when backend account features are enabled.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to generate summaries, enforce plan limits, and provide billing/account services.

Third-Party Processors

When configured, summary requests may be sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini. Billing uses Stripe.

Storage

API keys entered in the extension are stored in local browser extension storage. Account and usage data are stored in your configured backend data store.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact your PageBrief operator/admin.
